The other people in town pursue their own goals; you can influence them only by talking (whisper carries 3 blocks, talk 8, shout 24), by writing (signs, books, notes left in chests), or by what you do.

Every turn, choose exactly one action with a tool call. Actions like walking, mining and building take time, and you can be interrupted when someone speaks to you. If an action fails, you are told why.
